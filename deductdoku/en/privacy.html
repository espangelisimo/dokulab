DeductDoku Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Español · Terms of Service

In short

DeductDoku does not collect, send or sell personal data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

1. Controller

Cluelabs, the trading name of Ángel Velarde Naranjo (Spain). Contact: angel.velarde.naranjo@gmail.com.

2. What is stored and where

The game stores on your own device your progress, your settings (language, sound, accessibility) and, if you buy content, a flag saying it is unlocked. This data never leaves your device (except the optional copy in section 4) and never reaches Cluelabs. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings.

3. Google Play services

When you buy content or the game checks for an update, Google Play performs that operation with your Google account. Google processes that data as controller under its own privacy policy. Cluelabs only receives confirmation of the purchase, without your payment details.

This is why the game has internet permission: it uses it only to talk to Google Play (showing the price, completing or restoring the purchase, checking at start-up that it is still valid, and looking for updates). The game connects to no other server and can be played offline once unlocked.

4. Optional copy in your Google account

If you ask for it in Settings ("Cloud backup"), the game saves your progress (solved cases, stars, games in progress and settings) in the game's private space inside your own Google Drive. That space does not appear among your files and is used only by this game. Cluelabs does not receive or see that data: it goes from your phone to your Google account. You can delete it from the game ("Delete my cloud copy") or from your Google account settings, and sign out whenever you like. Without signing in the game works the same and everything stays on your device only.

Also, when the game thinks it is a good moment, it may ask for a review using the Google Play window: Google shows and manages it, and Cluelabs receives nothing from it.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to register or for your name, email or age.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers or share data with third parties.
  • We do not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

6. Children

Because the game collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

7. If you email us

If you send us an email, we use your address and what you tell us only to reply, and delete it when no longer needed. You may at any time ask to access, correct or delete that data, and complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, the AEPD).

8. Changes

If the game changes how it handles data, we will update this page before releasing that version.
